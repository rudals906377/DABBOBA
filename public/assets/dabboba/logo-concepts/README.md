# DABBOBA DBB Pixel Logo Concepts

All concepts explore one unified mark in which `D` transforms into `B`, with a second `B` implied through echo, negative space, or repeated structure. Golden-section relationships were used as proportional starting points, then adjusted for optical balance and small-size stability.

The canonical DABBOBA representative color is `#91E98E` in sRGB. Current vector exports use that value; older raster-only comparison concepts remain historical explorations until a final mark is selected.

1. `dbb-pixel-01-split.png` — split D-to-B outline with a lime transition joint
2. `dbb-pixel-02-shared-spine.png` — shared vertical spine and nested B counters
3. `dbb-pixel-03-hinge.png` — central hinge/fold transformation
4. `dbb-pixel-04-stencil.png` — heavy negative-space stencil
5. `dbb-pixel-05-capsule-hinge.png` — restrained upper/lower chamber split
6. `dbb-pixel-06-echo.png` — nested D/B echo system
7. `dbb-pixel-07-matrix.png` — modular square-matrix construction
8. `dbb-pixel-08-mirror-fold.png` — optically balanced mirror-fold D/B
9. `dbb-pixel-09-continuous-path.png` — continuous nested pixel path
10. `dbb-pixel-10-controlled-glitch.png` — five-step controlled transition into double B

`dbb-pixel-09-central-only.svg` and its PNG export isolate only concept 09's central D–B hinge. The surrounding D frame, repeated right rails, detached edge fragments, and background are removed. The mark uses a strict 8-unit stair-step grid, one lime hinge module, and a transparent square canvas with a small optical shift left and upward.

`dbb-line-09-central-overlap.svg` and its PNG export are the continuous-line revision. The pixel stairs are replaced by uninterrupted horizontal, vertical, and 45-degree vector segments. The D and B use one shared return segment from `(144,96)` through `(120,120)` and one shared center rail, preventing doubled antialiasing or stroke weight. The lime hinge remains centered precisely on the shared rail and B spine.

`dabboba-wordmark-dot.svg` and its PNG export contain only the exact uppercase text `DABBOBA`. Each letter uses the same 5×7 square-dot matrix, cap height, baseline, dot size, and advance width. The capitalization rhythm `DaBboBa` determines the accents: the first `D`, third-position `B`, and sixth-position `B` use canonical DABBOBA brand color `#91E98E`; the remaining `A–B–O–A` positions stay app ink `#111411`, with no extra icon or decoration.

The comparison board is `dbb-pixel-concepts-01-10.png`. Its top row is concepts 01–05 from left to right; its bottom row is concepts 06–10 from left to right.

These are raster concept explorations. After one direction is selected, redraw the chosen mark on a deterministic pixel grid as SVG and export app-icon, favicon, monochrome, light, and dark variants.

`dbb-pixel-reference-refined.png` is the reference-preserving option: the original overlapping D/B pixel construction is retained while stroke weight, stepped curves, negative space, margins, and optical balance are cleaned up.

`dbb-pixel-double-d-aligned.svg` and its PNG export use two exact copies of one uppercase pixel D. Both share the same cap height, baseline, dimensions, and stroke weight; the second D is moved horizontally by 160 units, approximately 38.5% of one D width, with no vertical offset.

`dabboba-wordmark-hybrid-01-source.png` is the selected app-wide wordmark direction. It replaces the full dot-matrix wordmark with a highly legible, bold geometric uppercase construction and retains only restrained stepped pixel terminals. The production transparent export is `../brand/dabboba-wordmark.png`; use it for splash, home, login, draw-header, and Expo loading surfaces.
